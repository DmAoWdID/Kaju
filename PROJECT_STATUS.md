## What's implemented

- **Kaiju data**: all 18 documented species, verbatim from `Docs/Kaju game.pdf`, with validation (missing fields, bad types, duplicate ids/names, KL range, 3 traits, stat totals). Ambiguities are logged in `Docs/IMPLEMENTATION_DECISIONS.md`.
- **World**: hub island with spawn → bridge → Wilds island with scenery; 8 identical personal park plots.
- **Wild kaijus**: a guaranteed Kragmor nearest the bridge, plus 3 KL-weighted random spawns. They wander, show a nameplate with a KL badge and a colored KL ground ring, and have an **E / tap prompt "Battle"**. They respawn after capture or defeat.
- **Turn-based battle**: server-validated start (distance, one battle per player, one player per wild kaiju). Pick your kaiju, then Attack / Defend / Save with action points. Has victory/defeat/draw, Retreat and an idle timeout.
- **3D battle arena** (Jurassic World: The Game style, client-only): both kaijus face off on a floating arena and act out each round (lunges, hits, damage numbers, shields, charge rings, knockouts, capture chains, cell dissolve). HP/AP plates at the top, actions at the bottom (D-15).
- **Reward choice**: Capture with chain (blocked with an explanation if the chain is too short) or Defeat for Kaiju Cells. Granted once, server-side.
- **Collection** panel: species, KL, level, threat, all stats, traits, park status, Level up (Kaiju Cells), **Fuse** at levels 10/20/30 (consumes an identical same-level duplicate, +1 level, max 40), Place in park / Remove.
- **Studio-only dev buttons** under the currencies (+1k Cells, +$1k). The server accepts them only in Studio.
- **Park**: starter Kragmor in a free Small enclosure. Build Small/Medium/Large enclosures on a 3×3 grid (Shop → Build → tap a glowing slot). Housed kaijus are shown in their enclosure. KL → enclosure size is enforced.
- **Kaiju animation** (client-side, procedural): legs walk in a gait synced to movement (diagonal pairs for quadrupeds, alternating for bipeds, rippling for Cataboulder), arms counter-swing, tails swish, wings flap, heads and necks nod, Jellqouia's tentacles sway. Idle motion when standing. Works for wild, park and battle-arena kaijus.
- **Economy**: money and Kaiju Cells HUD, income per housed kaiju every second, chain shop (4 tiers).
- **Save-ready data**: DataStore with session locking on live servers; memory-only in Studio (HUD says so).
- Desktop + touch: on-screen buttons for everything; ProximityPrompt works with E, gamepad X, and tap.

All balance numbers are provisional and centralized in `src/shared/Config/GameConfig.luau`.

## How to run

Tools on this machine: Rojo 7.7.1 at `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\rojo\rojo.exe`, Luau CLI at `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\luau\`. They are not on PATH.

**Automated checks** (Git Bash, repo root):
```bash
bash scripts/check.sh
```

The world is generated by the server at runtime, so the place needs an **empty Workspace**. The Baseplate template's Baseplate part and SpawnLocation would overlap the hub and compete with its spawn.

1. Build the place once: `bash scripts/check.sh` (or `rojo build default.project.json -o build/KaijuPark.rbxl`).
2. Open `build/KaijuPark.rbxl` in Roblox Studio.
3. For live sync while editing code, in PowerShell at the repo root run `& "$env:LOCALAPPDATA\Programs\rojo\rojo.exe" serve`, then in Studio go to Plugins → Rojo → **Connect** (localhost:34872).
4. Press **Play** (F5).

(Using an existing place instead: delete its `Baseplate` and `SpawnLocation` first.)

## Manual Studio test checklist

Open the **Output** window first.
1. Output shows `[KaijuPark] 18 kaiju species loaded`, `[DataService] persistence mode: memory …`, `[KaijuPark] server ready`, and no red errors.
2. HUD (top-left) shows $250, 0 Cells, "Starter Chain (up to KL 1)", "Test mode: progress is not saved". A welcome toast appears.
3. Walk north across the bridge, or press **Wilds**. **Kragmor** (grey crawler with crimson legs, green KL1 badge) is near the bridge end.
4. Walk close: the **"Battle"** prompt appears. Press **E** (or tap it).
5. The battle arena opens: wild Kragmor drops in on the right and the HP plates show at the top. Pick your Kragmor: it drops in on the left.
6. Use Save / Attack / Defend. Check the animations: gold ring for Save, blue shield for Defend, lunge plus red flash and damage number for Attack. HP drops on impact, AP pips update, and buttons are locked while a round plays. Attack is disabled at 0 AP. Win (tip: Save to 2–3 AP, then Attack): the wild kaiju tips over.
7. On **Victory**: choose **Capture** → chains wrap it → "Captured!" → Continue (the camera returns to your character). Open **Collection**: 2 Kragmors. The wild Kragmor disappears and a new one respawns ~8 s later.
8. Battle again and choose **Defeat** → +10 Kaiju Cells in the HUD. In Collection, **Level up** costs 10 Cells.
9. Try a KL2+ wild kaiju (if one spawned): after winning, Capture is disabled ("needs Reinforced Chain").
10. Press **My Park**: you are teleported to your plot, and the starter Kragmor is in a fenced Small enclosure labelled "+1 / sec". Money ticks up by 1 per second.
11. **Shop → Small Enclosure → Build**: glowing slots appear. Click/tap one: $100 deducted, enclosure appears.
12. **Collection → Place in park** on the captured Kragmor: it appears in the new enclosure; income becomes +2/s.
13. Retreat mid-battle, and leave a battle idle 2 min. In both cases the wild kaiju becomes available again.
14. Mobile: Studio → Test → Device emulator (phone): check the HUD, battle buttons and build tapping are usable.
15. Multiplayer: Test → Clients and Servers → 2 players. Each gets their own plot. Only one player can battle a given wild kaiju.

16. **Fusion** (Studio): capture a second Kragmor. Press **DEV +1k Cells** once, then level both Kragmors to 10 in Collection (450 Cells each). The level-10 button becomes **Fuse → Lv 11**: tap twice. Expected: one Kragmor at level 11, the duplicate gone, any enclosure it used now empty, a "Fusion complete!" toast, and the park nameplate showing Lv 11. Level up again with Cells to check 11→12.

Please report any Output errors. That is the fastest way to fix runtime issues I couldn't test.

## Known issues / limitations

- DataStore persistence only runs on live servers (or Studio with API access + `useDataStoreInStudio`); not yet tested there.
- Placeholder art only, but each of the 18 kaijus has a hand-built part model following its concept sheet (`KaijuDesigns.luau`, 24–225 parts each). Checked with an offline preview render, not yet in Studio. Real models can be dropped into `ReplicatedStorage.KaijuModels` (see `Docs/TECHNICAL_ARCHITECTURE.md`).
- Fusion gates at 10/20/30 are our interpretation of the GDD (see D-14). Fusion is unit-tested but **not yet play-tested in Studio**.
- Traits have no combat effect. Team size is 1.
- Enclosure placement uses a fixed 3×3 grid (no free placement, moving or selling yet).
- Only one Wilds island (GDD: seven islands around a landmass, ~20 wild kaijus).
- No offline income. Park kaijus can still be used in battle.
- `Docs/message.txt` is truncated (section 15 ends mid-sentence).
- Git: local repo on `main`, no remote configured yet.
